# Adaptive learning policies

## Diagnostic-first planning
Ask 5–10 short, representative questions covering high-priority concepts and suspected confusion pairs before committing to a study plan. Collect answers and optionally confidence (0–1); do not show the key early. Mark untested concepts unknown, not mastered. Diagnostic outcomes use the same grading and persistence path as quizzes. A guess or a correct answer without adequate reasoning is insufficient evidence of mastery. Use partial credit and label diagnostic uncertainty.

Use an explained heuristic: importance (1–5) × weakness (0.2–1, unknown 0.7) × confusion risk (1–2) / estimated study minutes. This is a scheduling aid, not a validated score. Blend overdue reviews and prerequisite gaps into the shortlist. Allocate within the student's actual available study budget, with a final review buffer; exam countdown and study budget are different. Do not drop important prerequisites solely because they take longer. Recompute after each answer.

## Error diagnosis and repair
| Error type | Evidence to seek | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| knowledge_gap | cannot explain definition | brief reteaching, basic recall |
| concept_confusion | uses another concept's rule | compare/contrast, discrimination question |
| retrieval_failure | knows after a cue | delayed unaided recall |
| application_failure | definition correct, transfer wrong | worked example, guided case, independent case |
| procedure_error | wrong step or calculation | isolate step, stepwise practice |
| misread_question | overlooks condition | identify requirements before solving |
| overconfidence_guess | unsupported answer or admitted guess | justify choice, confidence calibration |
| unknown | insufficient evidence | ask one diagnostic follow-up |

Do not infer guessing from a wrong answer alone. Store self-reported confidence as nullable. For partial or uncertain grading, explain the rubric and uncertainty. Save confusion pairs with both concept IDs and distinguish suspected from observed. Resolve suspected pairs only after discrimination practice; do not treat one correct answer as permanent mastery.

## Professor style profile
Save `.study/<course-id>/professor-profile.md`. For each pattern record evidence source, page/question identifier, sample count, and limitations. Analyze format distribution, preferred relationships, definition/application balance, calculation depth, distractors, wording and essay criteria.

Separate **Observed** (direct counts/examples), **Inferred** (qualified interpretation), and **Unknown** (unsupported dimensions). With no past exams, keep exam-pattern claims unknown; lecture emphasis can support a qualified inference only. Use confirmed scope over historical frequency. Generate original practice with supported content and observed style; never promise future exam questions.

## Time-based modes
Ask for exam date, time and timezone if ambiguous; do not invent a countdown. Use user-selected mode if explicitly requested. If no date is known, use Learn and label it provisional. Recompute mode at session start and when time information changes.

| Time remaining | Mode | Policy |
|---|---|---|
| 7 days or more | Learn | prerequisites, explanation, broad diagnostics, spaced practice |
| 24 hours to under 7 days | Practice | mixed formats, cases, weak topics, timed sets |
| 1 hour to under 24 hours | Exam | Critical/High, mistakes, confusion pairs, retrieval; avoid new low-priority topics |
| 0 to under 1 hour | Cram | must-know facts/formulas, mistakes, 5–10 rapid recalls; no long summaries |

If the exam has passed, ask whether to close it or plan another exam. Allow a short prerequisite explanation when essential in Exam/Cram mode. Compress diagnostics to 1–3 rapid checks in Cram mode or honor an explicit skip.

## Mock exams
Agree on time budget, scope and format. Match supported professor patterns; label unknown patterns. Present the set without solutions, collect answers, then grade against a prepared rubric. Record per-format performance, errors and optional timing; prioritize remaining weak spots. Do not claim to enforce a timer without host support. Keep answer keys hidden until submission.
