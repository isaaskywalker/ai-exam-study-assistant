# Difficulty Rules

## Basic
Definitions, terminology, simple factual recall, formula identification.

## Intermediate
Comparisons, interpretation, relationships, examples, distinguishing similar concepts.

## Advanced
Case/application, multi-concept reasoning, justification, transfer.

## Adaptive rule

Start at Basic or Intermediate based on source complexity.

After 2–3 consecutive correct answers:
- increase difficulty, or
- move to a related concept.

After 2 incorrect answers on the same concept:
- reteach briefly
- ask one simpler diagnostic question
- then return to the original difficulty.
